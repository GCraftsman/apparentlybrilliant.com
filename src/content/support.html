Support

Need help with an app? Send a note and include a few details so we can see what went wrong.

How to get help

Email michael@apparentlybrilliant.com with:

  • The name of the app
  • Your iPhone or iPad model, and the iOS version
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead

We read every message. This address is the support contact for Apparently Brilliant apps.

Questions

Where can I download the apps?

Nothing is on the App Store yet. When an app ships, it will be listed on the apps page with a link to Apple’s store.

Do the apps collect personal data?

By default, no. We design apps that do not require an account and do not collect personal information. The privacy policy describes that default. If a specific app ever works differently, its App Store privacy label will say so.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I found a bug, or I have an idea.

Please email us. A bug report is most useful with the details listed above. Ideas are welcome too.
